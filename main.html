More than a meal

The soul of a
morning table.

Nasi Lemak means “fatty rice”, a loving nod to rice simmered in coconut milk until each grain is rich, glossy, and deeply aromatic. It began as a humble Malay breakfast, but today it is enjoyed at any hour, from a paper-wrapped street-side packet to a carefully plated restaurant feast.

Its magic is in the balance: sweet coconut, punchy sambal, salty ikan bilis, cooling cucumber, and roasted peanuts. Every region and family has its own way of making it—and that is precisely what makes it feel like home.

Cook at home

Your perfect
nasi lemak.

Start with the essentials. This recipe makes enough for four generous plates and leaves plenty of room for your own favourite toppings.

⏱ 55 min total◉ Serves 4

Gather your ingredients

  • 012 cups jasmine rice
  • 021 cup coconut milk
  • 031 cup water + 1 tsp salt
  • 042 pandan leaves, knotted
  • 051 cup sambal (store-bought is fine)
  • 064 eggs, ikan bilis & peanuts

Make it yours

  1. 1
    Wash & infuse

    Rinse rice until the water runs clear. Add coconut milk, water, salt, and pandan leaves.

  2. 2
    Steam until fluffy

    Cook in a rice cooker or covered pot. Rest for 10 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork.

  3. 3
    Build the plate

    Fry the eggs, ikan bilis, and peanuts. Serve the warm rice with sambal and cool cucumber.
